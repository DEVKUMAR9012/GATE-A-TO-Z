📚 How to Find More Topper Data
GATE Overflow — AIR 1 Tag — Community posts where toppers self-disclose ranks/scores. Most reliable community-verified source.
Official GATE Statistics — IIT GATE websites publish year-wise qualifying marks, appeared/qualified counts. No individual names.
GATE Overflow YouTube — Topper interviews with verified rank holders who have chosen to go public.
Coaching Institute Toppers Lists (Made Easy, ACE, Unacademy) — Note: these are marketing materials and cannot be independently verified against official IIT records. Use with caution.
