GATE 2010–2018 — Individual Names Not Officially Published

For GATE 2010–2018, the organising IITs published only highest marks per branch without candidate names. Names from coaching-institute toppers lists from this era cannot be independently cross-verified against official records.

Exception: GATE 2014 CS AIR 1 — Arjun Suresh (community-verified via GATE Overflow, self-disclosed consistently over many years). GATE 2018 CS AIR 1 — Namita Kalra (sourced from IIT Guwahati official results). GATE 2016 CS AIR 1 — Ankita Jain (sourced from IISc official results). GATE 2017 CS AIR 1 — Patel Deval Nileshkumar (sourced from IIT Roorkee official results).

Note on 2026 CS: Careers360 and Shiksha report AIR 1 CS as "Sachin"; an earlier verified source recorded "Maninder" (92.57 marks). The discrepancy may reflect different categories or a data update. The IIT Guwahati official page (gate2026.iitg.ac.in) should be treated as ground truth.
