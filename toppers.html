What All Toppers Have in Common

📚 PYQ Obsession

Every single topper solved 10+ years of PYQs at minimum. Not once — multiple times. They treat PYQs as the ultimate source of truth.

📝 Handwritten Notes

All toppers maintained short, concise handwritten notes. Not 100-page notes — 1-2 page formula sheets per subject for last-week revision.

🧪 Mock Tests from November

Every topper started giving full-length mock tests in timed conditions by November. They spent equal time analyzing tests as giving them.
