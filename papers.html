GATE Papers & Combinations

CS (Computer Science & IT)

Primary CSE Paper

This is the natural choice for a B.Tech CSE student. It aligns perfectly with your college curriculum and unlocks conventional CSE M.Tech/MS programs and core PSU jobs.

Allowed Second-Paper Combinations

You can optionally appear for a second paper. For CS, allowed secondary options include: DA, EC, GE, MA, ME, PH, RA, and ST.

CS + DA (Data Science & AI) Highly Recommended
Shares ~50% syllabus overlap (Programming, DS, Algo, Math). Excellent choice if targeting specialized M.Tech programs in AI/Data Science at top IITs.
CS + MA (Math) / ST (Statistics) Moderate
Good only if you wish to pursue hardcore theoretical research in computational math or statistics.
⚠ Second Paper Warning A second paper does NOT automatically unlock every additional M.Tech program. Each institute independently decides which GATE paper they accept for their degrees.
