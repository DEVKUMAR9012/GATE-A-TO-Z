PSU Recruitment for CS/IT

⚠️ The Reality of PSUs for CSE Unlike Core branches (Mechanical/Electrical), CSE has significantly fewer PSU vacancies. A "Qualified" GATE status is NOT enough. To secure a PSU job through GATE CSE, you generally need an All India Rank (AIR) under 200 for General Category. Treat PSU preparation as a bonus, not a primary goal.

ONGC

Oil and Natural Gas Corporation

Oil & Gas Active
AIR < 100
Role Programming Officer / E1
CTC / Package ~₹ 17–20 LPA
Safe AIR (General) < 100
Eligibility B.Tech CSE/IT. Min 60% (55% for SC/ST/PH). Age ≤ 30 yrs.
Selection Process GATE Score → GD → Personal Interview (85% GATE + 15% PI)
One of the biggest PSU recruiters for CS. Excellent perks including HRA, quarters, and medical for entire family.

General Selection Process

StepStageWeightageTip
1GATE Score (Shortlisting)~85%This is the only thing you can control right now. Maximize your score.
2Apply on PSU Website—PSUs release their own notifications. Monitor their websites and job portals (like NaukriGovt) from Feb-April after GATE results.
3Group Discussion (GD)~5%Practice speaking on tech and current affairs for 2 minutes. Be the first to speak or summarize.
4Personal Interview (PI)~10-15%Review your B.Tech subjects deeply. Expect questions on OS, DBMS, Networks, and your Final Year Project.
