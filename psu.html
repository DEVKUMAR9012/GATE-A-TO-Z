PSU Recruitment for CSE

⚠ GATE Qualification does NOT guarantee a PSU job CSE students have fewer PSU vacancies compared to Electrical or Mechanical. Do not assume every PSU recruits CSE every year. Wait for official recruitment notifications. You typically need an AIR under 150 to secure an interview call.
