PSU Recruitment for Computer Science

⚠️ The Reality of PSUs for CSE Unlike Core branches (Mechanical/Electrical), CSE has significantly fewer PSU vacancies. A "Qualified" status is not enough. To secure a PSU job through GATE CSE, you generally need an All India Rank (AIR) under 200 for General Category.

Below is the exact list of Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) and Research Organizations that actively hire Computer Science graduates using GATE scores, along with historical expected rank cutoffs.

The Selection Process

  • Step 1: GATE Score - You must score in the top 0.1% to get shortlisted.
  • Step 2: Apply Independently - PSUs release their own notifications. You must apply on their respective websites using your GATE Registration Number.
  • Step 3: Group Discussion (GD) - Many PSUs conduct GDs to check communication skills and leadership.
  • Step 4: Personal Interview (PI) - Technical and HR rounds. Weightage is usually 85% GATE Score, 15% Interview/GD.
