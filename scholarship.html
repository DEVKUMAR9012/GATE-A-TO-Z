Finance

Stipends and Fellowships

One of the biggest advantages of qualifying GATE is the financial independence it offers for your higher studies.

The AICTE M.Tech Stipend

Standard Teaching Assistantship (TA)

₹ 12,400 / month

Any student who qualifies GATE and takes admission in an AICTE-approved institution (IITs, NITs, IIITs, and many State/Private universities) is entitled to this monthly stipend.

  • Duration: Valid for a maximum of 24 months.
  • Requirement: You must perform 8 hours of Teaching Assistantship (TA) work per week (evaluating papers, conducting lab sessions).
  • Condition: If you drop out of the M.Tech program, you may be asked to refund the stipend depending on the institute's rules.

The Elite PMRF (For PhD)

Prime Minister's Research Fellowship

₹ 70,000 - ₹ 80,000 / month

If you secure admission into a PhD or Direct-PhD program at an IIT, IISc, or top NIT, you can apply for the PMRF. It is highly prestigious and highly competitive.

  • Year 1 & 2: ₹ 70,000 per month
  • Year 3: ₹ 75,000 per month
  • Year 4 & 5: ₹ 80,000 per month
  • Contingency Grant: ₹ 2 Lakhs per year (for buying laptops, attending international conferences, etc.)
  • Eligibility: Requires a high GATE score (usually 750+) OR a CGPA > 8.0 from an IIT/NIT.

Standard PhD Fellowship (JRF/SRF)

If you do not get the PMRF but are admitted for a PhD (or MS by Research), you receive the standard MoE fellowship.

Phase Monthly Stipend Duration
Junior Research Fellow (JRF) ₹ 37,000 First 2 Years
Senior Research Fellow (SRF) ₹ 42,000 Next 3 Years (Subject to review)
