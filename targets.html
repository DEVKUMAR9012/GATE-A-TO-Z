Other Exams with GATE Syllabus

If your GATE exam doesn't go well, do not panic. The syllabus you studied makes you highly competitive for several other prestigious exams that happen shortly after GATE.

IIIT Hyderabad (PGEE)

April/May

Post Graduate Entrance Exam for IIITH. Arguably better placements than many new IITs (Avg CTC: 22+ LPA). Evaluates aptitude, discrete math, and core CS.

  • Requires high coding skills.
  • Syllabus heavily overlaps with GATE.

BITS HD (Higher Degree)

May

Entrance exam for M.E. at BITS Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad. BITS Pilani CS placements rival top NITs. The exam is slightly easier than GATE but focuses on speed.

  • Has a dedicated paper for Software Systems.
  • No negative marking for some sections (check latest notification).

BARC (Standalone Exam)

March/April

If you missed the GATE cutoff for BARC, they conduct their own online CBT. The syllabus is identical to GATE, but questions are more theoretical.

  • You only need to clear the cutoff to get an interview call.
  • Interview is brutal and purely technical.

ISRO Scientist/Engineer

Varies

ISRO conducts its own written test followed by an interview. It has 80 questions to be solved in 90 minutes. High speed and accuracy required.

  • Many questions are direct repeats from past GATE exams.
  • Interview weightage is 50%.

ISI Kolkata / CMI

May

Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) and Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI). Elite institutes for Data Science, Math, and CS.

  • Exam is highly mathematical (Discrete Math, Probability, Calculus).
  • Placements are consistently 25+ LPA.
