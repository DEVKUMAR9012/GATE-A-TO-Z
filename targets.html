Targets

CSE Rank Targets

Strategy Guidance, Not Official Cutoffs These are broad historical zones for General Category CS to help you plan. Category-wise reservations exist, and actual cutoffs shift every year based on difficulty and seat counts.
01

Just Qualify

Top 15,000 - 20,000 AIR. Yields a scorecard. Eligible for private/state colleges and basic stipends. No premier institutes.

02

Good NIT/IIIT

AIR 1,500 - 3,500. Realistic chance at mid-tier/newer NITs and IIITs via CCMT.

03

Strong NIT / New IIT

AIR 500 - 1,500. Top 3 NITs (Trichy, Surathkal, Warangal) and MS Research at older IITs.

04

Top IIT / IISc

AIR 100 - 500. Direct M.Tech at Top 7 IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Madras, etc.) and IISc Bangalore.

05

Elite / PSU

AIR 1 - 100. Direct interview calls for top PSUs (ONGC, IOCL). Utter dominance in IIT program choices.
