Syllabus, Weightage & Books

Understanding what to study is good, but knowing where the marks come from is how you hack the exam. Below is the historical 5-year average weightage and the standard reference books for GATE CSE.

Subject Avg. Marks (Out of 100) Priority Difficulty to Score
Engineering Math & Discrete Math 13 - 15 Marks Very High Medium
General Aptitude 15 Marks (Fixed) Very High Easy
Data Structures & Programming 10 - 12 Marks Very High Medium
Algorithms 6 - 8 Marks Very High Hard
Theory of Computation (TOC) 8 - 9 Marks High Easy (Once understood)
Operating Systems (OS) 8 - 10 Marks High Medium
Database Management (DBMS) 6 - 8 Marks High Easy
Computer Networks (CN) 8 - 10 Marks High Hard (Vast)
Computer Organization (COA) 8 - 10 Marks High Hard
Digital Logic 4 - 6 Marks Medium Easy
Compiler Design 4 - 5 Marks Medium Medium

Topic Details & Recommended Books

Programming & Data Structures

Topics: C programming, recursion, arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, BSTs, binary heaps, graphs.

Standard Book: "Data Structures and Algorithms made easy" by Narasimha Karumanchi (For quick GATE practice) OR "The C Programming Language" by Dennis Ritchie.

Algorithms

Topics: Searching, sorting, hashing, asymptotic complexity, greedy, dynamic programming, divide-and-conquer, graph traversals, MST, shortest paths.

Standard Book: "Introduction to Algorithms" (CLRS) by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, and Stein.

Engineering & Discrete Mathematics

Topics: Logic, sets, relations, functions, groups, graphs, combinatorics, linear algebra (matrices, eigenvalues), calculus, probability/statistics.

Standard Book: "Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications" by Kenneth H. Rosen. For Engineering Math: Higher Engineering Mathematics by B.S. Grewal.

Operating Systems

Topics: System calls, processes, threads, IPC, synchronization, deadlocks, CPU scheduling, memory management, virtual memory.

Standard Book: "Operating System Concepts" (Dinosaur book) by Abraham Silberschatz, Peter B. Galvin.

Databases (DBMS)

Topics: ER model, relational algebra, SQL, normal forms, indexing (B/B+ trees), transactions, concurrency control.

Standard Book: "Database System Concepts" by Abraham Silberschatz, Henry F. Korth, and S. Sudarshan OR Navathe.

Computer Networks

Topics: OSI/TCP-IP layers, switching, MAC, Ethernet, routing, IPv4, TCP/UDP congestion, DNS, HTTP.

Standard Book: "Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach" by Kurose and Ross, AND "Data Communications and Networking" by Forouzan.

Computer Organization & Architecture (COA)

Topics: Instruction set, ALU, control unit, memory hierarchy, cache, I/O, pipelining.

Standard Book: "Computer Organization and Embedded Systems" by Carl Hamacher.

Digital Logic

Topics: Boolean algebra, K-maps, combinational/sequential circuits, number representation.

Standard Book: "Digital Design" by M. Morris Mano.

Theory of Computation (TOC)

Topics: Regular expressions, DFA/NFA, CFG, PDA, Turing machines, undecidability.

Standard Book: "Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation" by Hopcroft, Motwani, and Ullman.

Compiler Design

Topics: Lexical analysis, parsing, syntax-directed translation, intermediate code, optimization.

Standard Book: "Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools" (Dragon Book) by Aho, Lam, Sethi, and Ullman.
