The Ultimate Free Resource List

You do not need to spend ₹50,000 on coaching to crack GATE CSE. The internet has everything you need for free, provided you know where to look. Here are the gold-standard resources.

Top YouTube Channels

Amit Khurana

Arguably the best free resource for Discrete Math, TOC, and Digital Logic. Extremely detailed lectures.

Visit Channel

Ravindrababu Ravula (RBR)

His older free videos on CN, OS, and DBMS are legendary for building intuition quickly.

Practice & PYQs (Previous Year Questions)

Resource Name Type Why it's essential
GATE Overflow (GO) PYQ Database The holy grail of GATE CSE. Contains every past GATE question with detailed community-driven answers. Do not use anything else for PYQs.
GO PDF Book Book A free, downloadable compilation of all GO questions categorized by subject and topic.
MadeEasy Test Series Mock Tests (Paid) The most standard mock test series in the country. Gives you an accurate representation of your relative rank.
Applied GATE (GO Test Series) Mock Tests (Paid) Known for highly conceptual and challenging questions that prevent you from just memorizing formulas.
⚠️ Warning about NPTEL While NPTEL lectures (by IIT professors) are incredibly deep and mathematically rigorous, they are not optimized for the GATE exam timeline. Only use NPTEL for specific topics you are struggling to understand, do not try to watch entire 40-hour NPTEL playlists for every subject.
