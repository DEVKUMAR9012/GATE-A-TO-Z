Basics

What is GATE?

The Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) is a national-level examination testing the comprehensive understanding of various undergraduate subjects in engineering and science. For 2027, it is organized by IIT Madras.

  • Eligibility: Candidates currently in the 3rd year or higher of an undergraduate degree program, or graduates.
  • Age Limit: No age limit.
  • Pattern: 3 hours, 65 questions, 100 marks. Contains MCQ, MSQ, and NAT questions.
  • Score Validity: 3 years from announcement of results (for academics). Usually 1 year for PSUs.
⚠ Important Warning Qualifying GATE does not guarantee admission, financial assistance, or a PSU job. It simply provides the score necessary to apply for these opportunities, which are governed by individual institute and employer criteria.

Marks vs Score vs Rank

Term Definition
Raw Marks Literal marks scored out of 100 in the exam.
Normalized Marks Adjusted marks to account for varying difficulty levels across multiple exam shifts (common for CS).
GATE Score The normalized score out of 1000. This is what institutes use for admissions via COAP/CCMT.
AIR (Rank) All India Rank - your absolute position among all CS test takers.
Qualifying Cutoff Minimum score needed to pass. E.g., ~30 marks for General category in CS. Getting this only gives a scorecard, not admission.
