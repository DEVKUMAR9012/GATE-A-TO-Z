Eligibility

You are eligible to appear for GATE if you are currently in the 3rd year or 4th year of any undergraduate degree program (B.Tech / B.E / B.Sc). Graduates can also apply with no age limit or attempt limit.

The Exam Pattern

GATE is a Computer Based Test (CBT) consisting of exactly 65 questions worth 100 marks. The paper is split into General Aptitude (15 marks) and Core Subject (85 marks).

Question Types

  • MCQ (Multiple Choice Questions): 4 options, only 1 correct. Carries negative marking (1/3rd for 1-mark Qs, 2/3rd for 2-mark Qs).
  • MSQ (Multiple Select Questions): 4 options, 1 or more correct. NO negative marking. ALL correct options must be selected to get marks (no partial credit).
  • NAT (Numerical Answer Type): Type the exact numerical answer using a virtual keyboard. NO negative marking.

The Annual GATE Timeline

Late August / Early September

Official Notification & Application Starts
The organizing IIT launches the official portal. Registration forms open for exactly one month.

First/Second Weekend of February

Exam Day
GATE CS is usually conducted on Saturday or Sunday. You will get a morning or afternoon slot.

Late February

Answer Key & Response Sheet
You can download your exact recorded responses and calculate your raw marks using the official answer key.

Mid-March

Results Declared
GATE Scorecard becomes available. You get your AIR, GATE Score (out of 1000), and Raw Marks.

April - July

Admissions & PSU Recruitment
COAP (IITs) and CCMT (NITs) rounds begin. PSUs release their individual application forms.
