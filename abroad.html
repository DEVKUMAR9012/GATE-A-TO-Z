Studying Abroad Using GATE

You don't always need the GRE to study abroad. A few top-tier global universities actively accept GATE scores for admission into their Master's programs.

⚠️ Language Proficiency is Still Required Even if a university waives the GRE in favor of GATE, you will almost certainly still need to provide a valid TOEFL or IELTS score to prove English proficiency.

Singapore (The Best Option)

The National University of Singapore (NUS) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU) are highly ranked globally (often Top 15 in the world for CS) and actively recruit Indian GATE scholars.

University Program GATE Score Req Funding
NUS (National Univ of Singapore) M.Comp / MS Research ~90 percentile+ Tuition Grant Scheme (Subsidized fees in exchange for working in SG for 3 years)
NTU (Nanyang Tech Univ) M.Eng / MS ~90 percentile+ Scholarships available for research-based degrees

Germany (The Affordable Option)

Public universities in Germany charge zero or negligible tuition fees. A few elite technical universities (TU9) accept GATE as a substitute for GRE for Indian applicants.

University Program GATE Score Req Note
TUM (Technical Univ of Munich) MSc Informatics / CS Recommended Having a good GATE score gives you extra points in their aptitude assessment framework.
RWTH Aachen MSc Data Science / CS Strongly Advised GATE is heavily considered for Indian applicants to prove core CS competency.

GRE vs GATE

Why use GATE?

  • Saves you the ₹18,000+ GRE exam fee.
  • You are already preparing for GATE technical subjects.
  • Shows core technical competency which European/Asian universities love.

Why write GRE instead?

  • GATE is NOT accepted in the USA or Canada.
  • If you want to go to US universities (Stanford, MIT, CMU), you must write the GRE.
