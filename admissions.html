IIT & NIT Admissions Guide

Securing a rank is only half the battle. You must understand how the decentralized IIT counseling (COAP) and centralized NIT counseling (CCMT) processes work. Below are the historical cutoffs for GATE CSE.

Understanding GATE Score vs Rank Cutoffs for admissions are almost always declared in terms of GATE Score (Out of 1000), not AIR (Rank) or raw marks. A score above 750 is considered excellent for Top IITs.

COAP: Common Offer Acceptance Portal (For IITs)

COAP is NOT an application portal. You must apply to each IIT individually on their respective websites. COAP is merely a dashboard where all IITs upload their offers so you can accept or reject them in real-time.

CCMT: Centralized Counseling (For NITs & IIITs)

Unlike IITs, you do not apply individually to NITs. You fill out a single preference form on the CCMT portal, and seats are allocated automatically across multiple rounds based on your GATE score.

M.Tech vs MS (Research)

M.Tech (Master of Technology)

  • Duration: Exactly 2 years.
  • Focus: Coursework and placements.
  • Cutoff: Very High (750+ at top IITs).

MS (Master of Science by Research)

  • Duration: 2.5 to 3 years.
  • Focus: Deep research and thesis work.
  • Cutoff: Comparatively lower (600+ at top IITs). Requires heavy interview/test prep.
Secret Hack: Interdisciplinary Branches Many students think they can only apply for M.Tech CSE. However, your GATE CS score makes you eligible for several highly-lucrative interdisciplinary branches at Top IITs. These branches often have lower cutoffs (650-700) but allow you to sit for the exact same software and data placements as core CSE students.
