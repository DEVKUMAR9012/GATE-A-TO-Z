GATE Score Calculator

Enter your expected marks and get your predicted GATE Score (out of 1000) and approximate All India Rank using the official IIT formula.

Official GATE Scoring Formula GATE Score = 350 + 650 × (M − Mq) / (M̄t − Mq)
Where M = your raw marks, Mq = qualifying cutoff (~25), M̄t = average marks of top 0.1% candidates (~80+). This calculator uses approximate reference values.
